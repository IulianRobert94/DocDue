DESCRIPTION (4000):

Ever discovered your car insurance expired last week? Or that your passport ran out just before a trip?

DocDue remembers every deadline for you — car documents, IDs, bills, rent, taxes, warranties — and reminds you in time.

NOTHING EXPIRES SILENTLY
• Reminders before, on the due day and after it — until you mark it done.
• IDs, passports and driving licences get a 6-month heads-up, so you can book an appointment.
• The app icon badge shows how many documents have expired.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Vehicles, Home, Personal, Financial — with dozens of preset types: insurance, inspections, road vignette, taxes, loans, subscriptions, warranties.
• Alerts grouped into overdue, next 7 days and next 30 days — with the amount due for each.
• A month calendar with every due date.

EASY TO USE
• Pick a type — insurance, inspection, ID, rent — and the recurrence and reminders are set for you.
• Scan the document with the camera or from your gallery — the expiry date and amount fill in automatically (on-device, no internet).
• Date shortcuts (+1 month, +1 year).
• Paid or renewed? One tap: see the new date, undo within 5 seconds.
• Pick the car or home from a list and see all its documents.

YOUR MONEY AT A GLANCE
• Payment history, a document health score, a currency per document (lei, euro, dollars).
• Analytics and a 12-month forecast of what's due (PRO).

MAINTENANCE LOG
• Note when you replace a part — battery, brake pads, tyres, filters — and see how long it lasted, what it cost and when the next one is due.

100% PRIVATE
Your data stays on your phone. No account, no servers, no ads. Face ID / fingerprint lock; with the lock on, notifications hide details on the lock screen.

Free for up to 10 documents. DocDue PRO — one-time purchase, no subscription: unlimited documents, analytics and forecast, backup and Excel export.
